# LENGTA — AI Working Guide

This file is the operating brief for AI assistants, designers, developers, and collaborators working on LENGTA.

## Source of truth
- Repository: https://github.com/01workspace/LENGTA
- Brand: LENGTA
- Tagline: WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.
- Brand philosophy: OWN THE BEAUTY.
- Audience: adults 18+.
- Treat the latest approved files in this repository as the source of truth.
- If a brief conflicts with an approved brand document, flag the conflict rather than silently changing the brand.

## Brand definition
LENGTA is a bold, seductive, provocative luxury women's intimate-fashion brand. Its personality is fearless, luxurious, fashion-forward, and unapologetic. The central philosophy is that a woman owns her beauty and makes her own choices.

## Identity boundaries
LENGTA is a twin brand of VYRA in broad category and business concept only. It must have its own:
- Logo and visual identity
- Product designs and collection names
- Campaign concepts and photography
- Brand voice and messaging
- Ecommerce experience and website

Never merge LENGTA with SAXI or ParisShoes. Do not copy another brand's approved creative work and relabel it as LENGTA.

## Creative direction
Explore considered, fashion-led designs such as luxury lingerie, corsetry, bodysuits, nightwear, intricate lace, sheer fabrics, dramatic cut-outs, sculptural straps, and crystal embellishment. Keep the result premium and intentional—not generic, cheap-looking, or dated.

Use professional fashion direction and clearly adult models. Present intimate apparel as fashion. Respect garment construction, fit, coverage, material behavior, and realistic product details.

## Visual identity
Use the following approved starting palette:
- Obsidian: #09090B
- Blood Wine: #5A1026
- Oxblood: #32101C
- Bone: #F2EAE2
- Antique Champagne: #B89A72

Do not invent a final logo, typeface license, or production-ready artwork. Those items remain to be selected and approved.

## Working rules
1. Be decisive and practical; avoid unnecessary questions.
2. Preserve approved creative decisions.
3. Separate concepts, drafts, and approved production assets clearly.
4. Do not publish, deploy, or modify a live website without explicit approval.
5. Before publishing, verify responsive behavior, links, product information, forms, accessibility basics, and checkout flow.
6. Do not invent product prices, materials, sizing, manufacturing claims, certifications, shipping promises, or legal approvals.
7. Keep customer data, credentials, API keys, and other secrets out of this public repository.

## Asset and file conventions
- Store editable source files where practical, alongside clearly named exports.
- Use descriptive filenames with version or date information when needed.
- Keep original/high-resolution campaign images; do not replace originals with compressed previews.
- Record image creator, license/permission, model release status, and usage restrictions for every production image.
- Put draft concepts in a clearly named draft area. Mark approved assets explicitly.
- Never claim an asset is in the repository unless its file is actually present.

## Decision status
Use these labels in documentation:
- **Approved** — explicitly approved for production.
- **Working direction** — current recommendation, still changeable.
- **Draft** — exploratory, not approved.
- **Needs validation** — requires commercial, legal, technical, or supplier confirmation.

## Open items
The following are not yet confirmed as production-ready:
- Final logo artwork and logo lockups
- Licensed typography and final type scale
- Final packaging artwork and print specifications
- Product technical packs, size chart, fit standards, and SKU structure
- Supplier, manufacturing, costing, and quality-control details
- Pricing, launch geography, shipping, returns, and legal policies
- Final ecommerce design, integrations, and launch approval
