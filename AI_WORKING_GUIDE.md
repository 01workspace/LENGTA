# LENGTA — AI Working Guide

This file is the operating brief for AI assistants, designers, developers and collaborators working on LENGTA.

## Source of truth
- Repository: https://github.com/01workspace/LENGTA
- Brand: LENGTA
- Tagline and central philosophy: OWN THE BEAUTY.
- Audience: adults 18+.
- Treat the latest approved files in this repository as the source of truth.
- If a brief conflicts with an approved brand document, flag the conflict rather than silently changing the brand.

## Brand definition
LENGTA is a bold, seductive, provocative luxury women's intimate-fashion brand. Its personality is fearless, luxurious, fashion-forward and unapologetic. A woman owns her beauty and makes her own choices.

## Identity boundaries
LENGTA is a twin brand of VYRA in broad category and business concept only. It must have its own logo and visual identity, product designs and collection names, campaign concepts and photography, brand voice and messaging, and ecommerce experience and website.
Never merge LENGTA with SAXI or ParisShoes. Do not copy another brand's approved creative work and relabel it as LENGTA.

## Creative direction
Explore considered, fashion-led designs such as luxury lingerie, corsetry, bodysuits, nightwear, intricate lace, sheer fabrics, dramatic cut-outs, sculptural straps and crystal embellishment. Keep the result premium and intentional—not generic, cheap-looking or dated.
Use professional fashion direction and clearly adult models. Present intimate apparel as fashion. Respect garment construction, fit, coverage, material behavior and realistic product details.

## Technical direction
- Frontend: Next.js App Router, React and TypeScript.
- Source control: this GitHub repository.
- Hosting: Vercel.
- Backend: Supabase, introduced only when required and configured securely.
- Keep secrets in platform environment variables; never commit credentials or .env files.
- Work on feature/migration branches and use pull requests. Do not publish production without explicit approval.

## Working rules
1. Be decisive and practical; avoid unnecessary questions.
2. Preserve approved creative decisions.
3. Separate concepts, drafts and approved production assets clearly.
4. Do not publish, deploy or modify a live website without explicit approval.
5. Before publishing, verify responsive behavior, links, product information, forms, accessibility basics and checkout flow.
6. Do not invent product prices, materials, sizing, manufacturing claims, certifications, shipping promises or legal approvals.
7. Keep customer data, credentials, API keys and other secrets out of this public repository.

## Asset conventions
Store editable source files where practical, alongside clearly named exports. Keep original/high-resolution campaign images; do not replace originals with compressed previews. Record image creator, license/permission, model release status and usage restrictions for every production image. Exclude assets belonging to other brands, including SAXI.

## Decision status
- **Approved** — explicitly approved for production.
- **Working direction** — current recommendation, still changeable.
- **Draft** — exploratory, not approved.
- **Needs validation** — requires commercial, legal, technical or supplier confirmation.

## Open items
Final logo lockups and licensed typography; product technical packs, size chart, fit standards and SKU structure; supplier/manufacturing/costing/quality control; pricing, launch geography, shipping, returns and legal policies; production imagery usage rights; ecommerce integrations and launch approval.
