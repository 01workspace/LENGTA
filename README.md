# LENGTA — OWN THE BEAUTY.

LENGTA is a bold, seductive, unapologetic luxury intimate-fashion brand for adult women who choose their own expression.

## Brand at a glance
- Category: premium women's intimate fashion.
- Character: bold, seductive, fearless, provocative, luxurious.
- Tagline and central philosophy: **OWN THE BEAUTY.**
- Audience: adults 18+.
- Visual world: dark editorial glamour, sculptural silhouettes and considered detail.
- Relationship to VYRA: twin-brand concept; independent identity, products, campaigns and website.

## Website stack
- Next.js App Router, React and TypeScript
- GitHub source control
- Vercel hosting and preview deployments
- Supabase planned for database, customer accounts, orders and storage

## Principles
1. Choice before provocation.
2. Fashion, not judgement.
3. Boldness with design intention.
4. Luxury through fit, construction, materials, finishing and service.
5. No body hierarchy.

Product specs, pricing, launch geography, manufacturing, legal clearance and final production assets require validation before commercial launch.
