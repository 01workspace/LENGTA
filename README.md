# LENGTA
## WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.

LENGTA is a bold, seductive, unapologetic luxury intimate-fashion brand for adult women who choose to be seen, set their own limits, and wear beauty on their own terms.

> Not made to please everyone. Made for the woman who owns her choice.

## Brand at a glance
- Category: premium women's intimate fashion.
- Character: bold, seductive, fearless, provocative, luxurious.
- Tagline: WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.
- Brand philosophy: OWN THE BEAUTY.
- Promise: Own your beauty. Own your choice.
- Audience: adult women drawn to expressive, daring fashion.
- Visual world: dark editorial glamour, sharp contrast, sculptural silhouettes.
- Relationship to VYRA: twin-brand concept; independent identity and expression.

## Principles
1. Choice before provocation.
2. Fashion, not judgement.
3. Boldness with design intention.
4. Luxury through fit, construction, materials, finishing, and service.
5. No body hierarchy.

This is the foundational strategy. Product specs, pricing, launch geography, manufacturing, legal clearance, and final logo artwork require validation before commercial launch.
