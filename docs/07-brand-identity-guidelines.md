# LENGTA Brand Identity Guidelines
**WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.**  
*Foundation edition · 2026*

> A house of provocative luxury.

## 1. Brand essence

**Brand:** LENGTA  
**Tagline:** WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.
**Brand philosophy:** OWN THE BEAUTY.  
**Category:** Luxury women's intimate fashion  
**Audience:** Adults 18+, with a core fashion customer aged approximately 25–40.  
**Positioning:** A provocative luxury label for women who choose how they express their beauty.

### Central belief
Beauty is not permission granted by someone else. It is personal power, self-expression and choice. LENGTA creates pieces for women who define their own boundaries and own the way they are seen.

### Brand promise
Distinctive, beautifully constructed intimate fashion that feels considered, confident and unapologetically individual.

### Brand idea
**Luxury with attitude.** Seduction through design, not excess. Confidence through choice, not approval.

## 2. Positioning and personality

LENGTA sits where couture sensibility meets intimate-fashion provocation. It should feel like a fashion house with a clear point of view—not a generic lingerie retailer.

Personality pillars:
- **Bold:** Makes a clear visual statement.
- **Seductive:** Uses suggestion, texture and silhouette rather than obviousness.
- **Fearless:** Never apologizes for confident design.
- **Luxurious:** Quality, fit, finish and restraint are non-negotiable.
- **Fashion-forward:** Leads with silhouette, styling and editorial imagination.
- **Self-possessed:** The woman is the author of her own image.

LENGTA should avoid mass-market lingerie clichés and overly delicate, bridal-coded luxury. Its signature is dark, architectural glamour with warmth, sensuality and a strong fashion point of view.

## 3. Visual identity

### Core colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian | `#09090B` | Primary dark, typography and foundations |
| Blood Wine | `#5A1026` | Signature accent and campaign colour |
| Oxblood | `#32101C` | Depth and secondary dark |
| Bone | `#F2EAE2` | Light backgrounds and negative space |
| Antique Champagne | `#B89A72` | Metallic accents, foil and details |

Use Obsidian and Bone as the structural foundation. Blood Wine and Oxblood bring signature depth. Antique Champagne is an accent, never a dominant fill. Avoid bright pink, generic rose-gold gradients and excessive metallic effects.

### Typography direction
- **Display:** High-contrast editorial serif with couture character. Suggested starting points: Cormorant Garamond or Bodoni Moda.
- **Supporting:** Neutral modern sans serif for clarity. Suggested starting points: Manrope or Helvetica Neue.
- **Usage:** Large editorial headlines, controlled letter spacing, concise labels and highly readable product information.
- Confirm font licensing before commercial deployment.

### Logo system
- **Primary:** Custom LENGTA wordmark in a refined high-contrast serif, uppercase and carefully spaced.
- **Secondary:** Compact L monogram derived from a folded-ribbon or interlocking-couture-strap gesture.
- **Lockup:** Wordmark with WEAR YOUR BEAUTY. beneath it, used for brand moments rather than every product touchpoint.
- The final logo must be custom-drawn and optically corrected. A typed font is only a prototype, not the finished mark.

### Logo usage
Prefer Bone on Obsidian, Obsidian on Bone, or restrained Champagne foil on dark packaging. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the L. Never stretch, outline, add drop shadows, place over busy imagery or recreate the logo with an arbitrary font.

## 4. Art direction

### Photography
LENGTA imagery is adult luxury fashion photography. The garment is the hero: fit, construction, material, silhouette and distinctive details must be legible. Cast adult models with varied appearances and body types. Direct with confidence and composure; avoid objectifying or overly explicit presentation.

### Lighting and composition
Use sculpted directional light, deep shadow, tactile highlights and editorial negative space. Mix full looks, three-quarter portraits, precise product crops, fabric macros and construction details. Skin tones must remain natural; retouching should preserve realistic texture.

### Materials and styling
Prioritize fine lace, satin, mesh, velvet, structured fabrics, embroidery, sculptural straps and considered hardware. Crystal embellishment is selective and purposeful. Use dark studio sets, architectural interiors, rich drapery, stone, smoked glass and warm metallic accents. Props and sets should support the silhouette rather than compete with it.

## 5. Product and collection architecture

Core categories:
1. Signature lingerie sets
2. Corsetry, bustiers and structured pieces
3. Sculptural bodysuits
4. Sheer and statement intimate fashion
5. Nightwear and robes
6. Limited-edition occasion pieces

Build focused capsules with a clear concept, signature material, colour story and hero silhouette. Establish recognizable carry-over signatures, then introduce seasonal experimentation. Each collection should feel distinct without breaking the LENGTA visual language.

**Quality principles:** Fit and comfort are part of luxury. Prioritize grading across sizes, supportive construction where needed, durable closures, clean finishing, fabric testing and honest care guidance. Do not make unsupported claims about sustainability, origin or performance.

## 6. Voice and messaging

**Voice:** Confident, concise, sensual, intelligent. Never apologetic, crude, juvenile or over-explained.

Message examples:
- **WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.**
- Made to be yours.
- Elegance with an edge.
- Your presence. Your rules.
- Designed for the way you define yourself.

Writing rules:
- Use short statements and strong verbs.
- Avoid empty superlatives, fake exclusivity and body-shaming language.
- Never suggest women dress for someone else's approval.
- Speak to choice, craft, confidence and individuality.

## 7. Customer experience

### Packaging
Primary packaging: rigid Obsidian box with restrained Champagne foil wordmark. Interior: Bone tissue with a subtle repeating monogram. Secondary packaging: matte Blood Wine bag or mailer, discreet exterior branding and premium protective wrapping. Include a care card and concise brand note.

### Labels and trims
Use a clean woven label with the wordmark, soft-touch finishes against skin and discreet size/care information. Hardware should feel intentional and durable. Branding should never compromise comfort.

### Ecommerce
A mobile-first luxury storefront with editorial storytelling and effortless shopping. Homepage: campaign-led hero, collection entry points, bestsellers, brand philosophy and considered editorial content. Product pages: clear imagery, fit and size guidance, material details, care, delivery and returns. Checkout must be simple, secure and transparent.

### Customer service
Warm, discreet, knowledgeable and respectful. Treat sizing and intimate-product questions without embarrassment. State hygiene-related return rules clearly and fairly before purchase, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Brand applications

Social templates should use strong image-led compositions, minimal copy and consistent typography. Keep product launches, campaign imagery, behind-the-scenes craft and styling education within one visual system.

Apply the identity consistently to business cards, garment tags, tissue, boxes, shopping bags, shipping inserts, event invitations and showroom signage.

## 9. Production checklist

### Established in this foundation edition
- Brand strategy, positioning and personality
- Palette and typography direction
- Logo and monogram design brief
- Art direction and photography principles
- Product architecture and collection principles
- Voice and messaging
- Packaging, ecommerce and customer-experience principles

### Still to produce and approve
1. Custom vector logo and monogram.
2. Final licensed typography and complete usage specifications.
3. Packaging dielines and production-ready artwork.
4. Garment label and trim artwork.
5. Campaign photography and product image library.
6. Ecommerce UI design and implementation.
7. Final legal, care, sizing, shipping and returns copy for launch markets.

## 10. Brand governance

LENGTA is an independent identity. It may share a broad intimate-fashion universe with VYRA, but must never reuse VYRA's logo, campaign concepts, website design or messaging. Keep LENGTA distinct from SAXI and ParisShoes.

**Brand guardrails:** Choice before provocation. Fashion, not judgement. Boldness with design intention. Luxury through fit, construction, materials, finishing and service. No body hierarchy.
