# LENGTA — Brand Kit & Visual Identity

## Identity direction
**Provocative luxury / dark editorial glamour.**

The visual system should feel like a fashion-house campaign with an edge: controlled darkness, sculptural light, rich texture, confident typography, and compositions that let the garment lead.

## Logo direction
Create a custom uppercase LENGTA wordmark.
- Start with a high-contrast editorial serif or sharply tailored modern display face.
- Explore a distinctive G or A detail as a recognizable signature.
- Keep letter spacing deliberate.
- Avoid generic script, decorative flourishes, and obvious lingerie icons.
- Develop a compact monogram only after the wordmark is approved.
- Required lockups: primary wordmark, compact monogram, black, ivory, and social avatar.

Final vector artwork and trademark/domain clearance are not yet completed.

## Typography
Use licensed fonts or open-source alternatives after checking licences.
- Display and campaign headlines: Bodoni Moda or Cormorant Garamond.
- Body, ecommerce, and product details: Inter or Manrope.
- Utility labels: Inter Medium with controlled uppercase tracking.

Headlines can be expressive; product information must remain highly readable.

## Photography and art direction
**Light:** hard directional light, sculpted shadows, glossy highlights, low-key studio setups, occasional direct flash. Preserve real skin texture.

**Composition:** confident framing, negative space, strong geometry, close-up material studies, full-garment views, unexpected crops. The garment remains the hero.

**Styling:** fashion editorial rather than generic boudoir. Pair intimate pieces with tailoring, outerwear, statement accessories, or architectural styling where appropriate.

**Model direction:** adult models, a range of body shapes, skin tones, and personal styles. Direct for presence and agency—not passive objectification. Maintain realistic anatomy and garment fit.

**Environments:** dark studio, architectural interiors, backstage fashion energy, concrete, polished metal, deep velvet, and controlled red-lit sets.

**Avoid:** plastic-looking skin, obvious AI artifacts, over-retouching, distorted anatomy, generic boudoir clichés, clutter, and imagery implying every customer must wear revealing pieces in public.

## Materials and tactile cues
Explore fine mesh, stretch lace, satin, power mesh, velvet accents, metallic hardware, embroidery, and crystal details. Confirm skin-contact safety, wash performance, opacity, stretch recovery, and supplier claims before use.

## Graphic language
Strong black/ivory contrast; deep red as a decisive accent; fine rules; tight grids; oversized editorial type; restrained metallic details; generous negative space.

## Packaging
Matte Obsidian base, Bone typography, a small Blood Wine detail, and tactile finishing. Unboxing should feel private, considered, and premium. Packaging must protect garments and communicate care clearly.

## Digital design
Dark-first editorial homepage with a clear shopping path. Product imagery should include consistent crops, detail views, and fit views. Provide a prominent size guide, fabric/care information, shipping/returns, and privacy-conscious checkout.

Accessibility requirements: strong contrast, keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text, readable type, and reduced-motion support.
