# LENGTA — Visual Identity System
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Working direction — not approved for production  
**Scope:** Visual identity specification; no changes to established brand strategy or palette.

## 1. Creative thesis
LENGTA is a house of provocative luxury. Its visual language should feel architectural, sensual, controlled and unmistakably fashion-led. The tension is deliberate: hard geometry against tactile softness; deep shadow against warm skin and Bone; precise typography against expressive silhouettes.

The woman is the author of the image. The garment—not exposure—is the visual hero.

## 2. Identity principles
1. **Architectural sensuality:** build compositions around line, structure, silhouette and negative space.
2. **Controlled provocation:** use suggestion, contrast and styling; avoid gratuitous exposure.
3. **Material credibility:** lace, satin, mesh, velvet, embroidery and hardware must look tactile and physically plausible.
4. **Editorial restraint:** one strong idea per frame; avoid clutter, decorative excess and generic boudoir cues.
5. **Wearer agency:** direct adult models with confidence and presence, never passive objectification.

## 3. Colour system
The five established core colours remain unchanged:
- Obsidian — `#09090B`: primary ground, typography and packaging.
- Blood Wine — `#5A1026`: signature accent and campaign moments.
- Oxblood — `#32101C`: depth, tonal layering and secondary panels.
- Bone — `#F2EAE2`: light ground, editorial type and contrast.
- Antique Champagne — `#B89A72`: restrained detail, rules, hardware and foil.

### Application guidance
- Use Obsidian and Bone as the structural pair.
- Blood Wine should be a deliberate focal accent, not a constant background.
- Oxblood creates tonal depth; do not substitute it for Obsidian in every dark application.
- Antique Champagne should occupy small areas and must not become a bright gold effect.
- Do not introduce new brand colours without review.

### Contrast
For digital interfaces, validate text/background combinations against WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not use Antique Champagne for small text on Bone or low-contrast dark-on-dark combinations. Use Bone or an accessible neutral for essential text on dark surfaces.

## 4. Typography system
**Display direction:** high-contrast editorial serif with couture character. Candidate families already identified in the brand kit: Cormorant Garamond or Bodoni Moda.  
**Supporting direction:** neutral contemporary sans serif. Candidate families: Manrope or Inter.

Selection is pending licensing, glyph coverage, rendering tests and a final specimen review. No typeface is approved by this document.

### Hierarchy
- Campaign headline: expressive serif, large scale, short copy.
- Collection title: serif display, compact and confident.
- Product title: clean sans serif, medium weight.
- Product data, sizing and care: highly legible sans serif; never sacrifice clarity for styling.
- Utility labels: uppercase sans serif with modest tracking.

Use sentence case for customer-facing explanations. Reserve all caps for brand marks, short campaign statements and navigation labels.

## 5. Wordmark and monogram brief
### Primary wordmark
- Custom uppercase LENGTA lettering.
- High-contrast editorial serif or sharply tailored display construction.
- Optical spacing, bespoke kerning and carefully resolved G and A forms.
- Must remain legible at small digital sizes and in one-colour reproduction.

### Signature exploration
Explore a subtle bespoke cut or terminal in the G or A. The signature must feel typographic and ownable—not like a generic lingerie symbol.

### Monogram
Develop only after the wordmark direction is selected. Explore a compact L derived from a folded ribbon, interlocking strap or architectural seam. Avoid literal corset, lips, bow or lingerie pictograms.

### Required lockups
1. Primary wordmark.
2. Wordmark with OWN THE BEAUTY. for brand moments.
3. Compact monogram.
4. One-colour dark and light versions.
5. Small social avatar.

All artwork remains draft until custom vector construction, legibility checks and legal clearance are complete. A typed font treatment is a prototype, not a final logo.

## 6. Graphic language
- Strong grid and generous negative space.
- Fine rules and deliberate alignment.
- Oversized editorial typography used sparingly.
- Cropping that emphasizes silhouette, seams, lace pattern and construction.
- Champagne details used as a fine accent only.
- No gratuitous gradients, glow effects, ornamental frames or generic luxury flourishes.

## 7. Photography direction
### Lighting
Low-key directional studio light, sculpted shadows, controlled highlights and occasional direct flash. Preserve realistic skin texture and garment material response.

### Composition
Every collection story should include:
- A full-garment or full-look image that clearly communicates silhouette.
- A three-quarter editorial portrait.
- A construction or fabric macro.
- A detail image showing closures, straps, lace or finishing.
- A clean ecommerce view with accurate colour and fit.

### Casting and model direction
Models must be clearly adult (18+). Cast varied adult appearances and body types. Direct with self-possession, strength and agency. Avoid sexualized camera angles or styling that makes the model appear underage.

### Styling and set
Use dark architectural studios, stone, smoked glass, polished metal, deep drapery and controlled wine-red environments. Tailoring and outerwear may frame intimate pieces as fashion. Props must support the garment, never compete with it.

### Retouching and quality control
Preserve anatomy, skin texture, realistic fabric behavior, garment construction and consistent colour. Reject distorted hands, impossible seams, melted hardware, inconsistent straps, implausible closures, excessive smoothing and colour shifts.

## 8. Ecommerce application
- Dark-first editorial entry, but keep shopping tasks clear and fast.
- Use Bone surfaces where product comparison, size selection or long-form reading benefits from brightness.
- Product cards should prioritize accurate garment images, product name, price and availability.
- Product detail pages need front/back/detail imagery, fit notes, size guidance, materials, care, delivery and returns.
- Buttons and focus states must be accessible and visibly interactive.
- Support keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text, reduced motion and responsive layouts.

## 9. Packaging expression
Working direction: matte Obsidian, Bone typography, restrained Blood Wine detail and a small Champagne foil application. Prioritize tactile quality, garment protection, discreet delivery and clear care information. Dielines, substrates, print finishes and supplier specifications remain unvalidated.

## 10. Asset production checklist
For each approved production image, record:
- Creator / photographer and creation date.
- Original master filename and resolution.
- Model age confirmation and model release.
- Copyright ownership or written usage licence.
- Allowed channels, territory, duration and paid-media rights.
- Retouching and colour-correction status.
- Approval status and approver.

Keep references, generated drafts, selected drafts and approved production assets in separate folders. Never label an image approved without explicit review.

## 11. Approval gates
This document is a **working direction**, not a production approval. Before production:
1. Review and select display/supporting typefaces and confirm licences.
2. Develop at least three custom wordmark routes.
3. Review the wordmark at large, small, monochrome and reversed sizes.
4. Develop the monogram from the selected wordmark.
5. Test colour and accessibility in digital UI.
6. Produce one campaign art-direction test and one product-image test.
7. Obtain explicit brand-owner approval before marking any asset approved.

## 12. Non-negotiable guardrails
- Preserve the established five-colour palette.
- Keep LENGTA visually independent from VYRA, SAXI and ParisShoes.
- Do not copy third-party logos, campaigns or artwork.
- Do not claim manufacturing, material, sustainability or performance attributes without evidence.
- Do not publish, deploy or represent draft assets as approved.
