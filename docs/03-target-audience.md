# LENGTA — Target Audience

## Primary audience
Adult women approximately 25–40 who are fashion-aware, digitally engaged, and comfortable using style to express identity. This age range is a planning hypothesis, not an eligibility boundary; all marketing and products are for adults 18+.

### Mindset
- Enjoys bold fashion and does not want her choices reduced to other people's opinions.
- Values confidence, individuality, and quality.
- Is curious about statement lingerie, corsetry, bodysuits, nightwear, and styling-led intimate fashion.
- Wants pieces that feel special, considered, and worth investing in.
- May choose daring designs for herself, a partner, a special occasion, or a visible fashion look—but does not need to justify the choice.

## Secondary audiences
- 21–24: emerging fashion shoppers drawn to expressive design; price sensitivity and education may be higher.
- 41–50+: confident, quality-focused customers who value fit, sophistication, and distinctive design. Do not assume age determines daringness.

## Psychographic segments
### The Statement Maker
Wants a striking piece with an unmistakable silhouette. Responds to strong campaign imagery and limited-edition designs.

### The Private Powerhouse
Buys primarily for herself. Wants tactile luxury, fit, comfort, and confidence even when no one else sees the garment.

### The Fashion Stylist
Treats intimate pieces as part of a broader wardrobe. Interested in layering, tailoring, bodysuits, visible straps, and styling versatility.

### The Collector
Looks for exceptional details, craftsmanship, special colors, and pieces that feel distinctive rather than mass-market.

## Customer needs
- Beautiful design that feels different from mainstream options.
- Reliable fit guidance and honest product photography.
- Inclusive size and fit information.
- Comfortable construction, secure closures, and suitable materials.
- Discreet, trustworthy fulfilment and clear return/exchange policies.
- Confidence that the brand respects personal boundaries and privacy.

## Purchase motivations
Self-expression, self-purchase, special occasions, gifting, wardrobe experimentation, fashion styling, collecting distinctive designs, and quality that justifies premium pricing.

## Barriers
Sizing uncertainty; fear that daring design means uncomfortable construction; photos that do not show real fit or coverage; unclear fabric/care/delivery/returns; privacy concerns.

**You choose the statement. LENGTA gives you the design.**
