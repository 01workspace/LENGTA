# LENGTA — Launch Roadmap

This is a practical starting sequence. Timelines and budgets should be set after geography, supplier capacity, and launch scale are confirmed.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Confirm brand name pronunciation, legal entity, trademark availability, and domain/social handle availability.
- Approve final wordmark and monogram.
- Confirm launch country, customer service language, currency, and shipping regions.
- Define price architecture using real cost, margin, and competitor research.
- Identify manufacturing partners and request samples.

Gate: no public commercial claims or final packaging until name clearance and product feasibility are checked.

## Phase 2 — Product development
- Select a focused first capsule.
- Create technical packs, measurement specs, grading, and construction requirements.
- Fit-test samples on a representative range of adult bodies.
- Verify fabric composition, colorfastness, wash performance, hardware, and skin-contact suitability.
- Finalize size chart, care instructions, and quality-control checklist.

Gate: every launch SKU must pass fit and quality review.

## Phase 3 — Brand and commerce
- Build a distinct LENGTA storefront; do not copy VYRA's visual identity.
- Create product pages with accurate imagery, fit notes, and full product information.
- Publish shipping, returns, privacy, payment, and customer support policies.
- Test checkout, order notifications, mobile performance, accessibility, and analytics.
- Use discreet packaging and privacy-conscious fulfilment.

Gate: complete test orders and customer support scenarios before launch.

## Phase 4 — Campaign
- Produce a hero editorial campaign with adult models and realistic garment fit.
- Create detail-led product imagery for ecommerce.
- Develop short-form video focused on construction, styling, movement, and confidence.
- Prepare launch email and social content.
- Work only with aligned partners and disclose paid partnerships.

Gate: review imagery for accurate product representation, consent, and brand fit.

## Phase 5 — Launch and learn
Track conversion, add-to-cart, product-page engagement, size exchanges, fit-related returns, satisfaction, repeat purchase, gross margin, fulfilment, and which product details customers respond to.

Use feedback to improve sizing, construction, imagery, and assortment—not to pressure customers into more revealing styles.

## Decisions still required
Final logo and pronunciation; launch geography and legal review; target price tier and first-capsule budget; supplier and manufacturing approach; initial size range and fit model; ecommerce platform, fulfilment, and returns policy; launch date and campaign budget.
