# LENGTA — Logo Development Directions

Version: 1.0  
Status: Concept exploration — not approved for production  
Branch: creative/logo-directions-v1  
Parent direction: docs/08-visual-identity-system-v1.md

## Objective

Develop a distinctive luxury-fashion identity for LENGTA that expresses the philosophy **OWN THE BEAUTY.** The identity must feel confident, seductive, editorial and enduring. It must work across ecommerce, campaign imagery, garment labels, woven tags, packaging, hardware, social avatars and digital interfaces.

The logo must identify a fashion house, not a generic lingerie retailer. Avoid literal lingerie illustrations, wings, crowns, lips, hearts, generic female silhouettes, ornamental scripts and stock luxury tropes.

## Three creative routes

### Route 01 — Couture Serif

**Role:** Primary recommended route.

A custom uppercase serif wordmark with refined high contrast, elegant proportions and deliberate optical spacing. The overall impression is composed, sensual and assured rather than decorative.

Design exploration:
- Custom-drawn uppercase LENGTA lettering.
- Editorial serif construction with controlled contrast and crisp terminals.
- Explore a memorable treatment of the G or A, but preserve immediate readability.
- Carefully tuned kerning, especially around EN, GT and TA.
- Tagline set separately in restrained uppercase sans or small caps with generous tracking.

Strengths:
- Strong fashion-house credibility.
- Excellent fit with dark editorial campaigns and premium packaging.
- Likely to remain relevant beyond seasonal trends.

Risks to resolve:
- Avoid looking like an off-the-shelf fashion serif.
- Ensure the wordmark remains legible at small garment-label sizes.
- Validate distinctiveness against existing fashion marks before production.

### Route 02 — Architectural Modern

A contemporary uppercase sans-serif wordmark built from precise geometry, generous spacing and subtle custom letterforms.

Design exploration:
- Clean, controlled strokes and balanced proportions.
- Consider a bespoke G, A or crossbar detail as a recognizable signature.
- Avoid overly thin strokes that disappear in embroidery, foil stamping or mobile UI.
- Keep the tagline subordinate and highly legible.

Strengths:
- Modern, direct and versatile.
- Strong performance in digital environments and contemporary product hardware.

Risks to resolve:
- Could feel too clinical or generic without a distinctive custom detail.
- Must retain sensuality and warmth through proportion and material application.

### Route 03 — Signature Monogram

A compact emblem derived from a custom L, or a carefully integrated L/G construction, intended to become a secondary brand asset.

Design exploration:
- Develop an original silhouette with a couture strap / folded-ribbon gesture only if it remains simple and reproducible.
- Test standalone, inside a restrained frame, and as a blind emboss.
- Do not force a complicated letter fusion simply to make the symbol look ornate.
- Pair with the full LENGTA wordmark; the monogram is not a substitute for the name during early brand building.

Strengths:
- Useful for garment hardware, clasps, zipper pulls, labels, packaging seals and social avatars.
- Can become a recognizable brand signature.

Risks to resolve:
- Letter construction must be unmistakable and ownable.
- Avoid resemblance to common luxury monograms or existing marks.
- Must reproduce cleanly at very small sizes.

## Recommended identity architecture

1. **Primary:** Approved feminine line-art emblem with refined LENGTA wordmark.
2. **Secondary:** Custom Signature Monogram, developed from the approved wordmark's letterform logic.
3. **Tagline lockup:** LENGTA / WEAR YOUR BEAUTY.
4. **Utility:** A restrained wordmark-only version for small digital and garment applications.

Do not combine all elements into one mandatory lockup. The identity must have flexible responsive versions.

## Evaluation criteria

Score each refined route against:
- Distinctiveness and ownability.
- Immediate readability.
- Luxury-fashion credibility.
- Reproduction at small sizes.
- Performance in one colour.
- Suitability for embroidery, woven labels, foil, embossing and hardware.
- Digital legibility and responsive use.
- Alignment with WEAR YOUR BEAUTY. and the OWN THE BEAUTY. philosophy.

## Required design deliverables before approval

- Three refined wordmark routes shown at equal scale.
- Black/white and palette applications for each.
- Clear-space and minimum-size tests.
- Monogram exploration and reduction tests.
- Small-size tests for woven labels and social avatars.
- Final selected artwork in editable vector format (SVG and PDF; source file where applicable).
- Font and asset licensing record.
- Basic logo usage sheet covering approved lockups, colourways, spacing and prohibited alterations.

## Approval gates

1. Review the three routes as concepts.
2. Select one route or request a focused refinement.
3. Refine custom letterforms and spacing.
4. Check distinctiveness and relevant trademark conflicts before commercial release.
5. Produce and inspect vector masters and application tests.
6. Obtain explicit user approval before marking any logo as final or moving it into production.

## Current decision

The brand owner approved the supplied feminine fine-line silhouette and LENGTA wordmark composition as the visual direction. Preserve that design. The remaining work is technical vector refinement, outlined lettering, export production and reproduction testing—not a new concept selection.
