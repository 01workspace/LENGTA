# LENGTA — Brand Voice & Messaging

## Voice
LENGTA speaks with confidence, tension, and control. It is provocative without being crude, seductive without begging, and luxurious without sounding distant.

Traits: direct, fearless, sensual, self-possessed, and respectful.

## Core message
**OWN THE BEAUTY.**

The woman is the owner of her beauty and her decisions. LENGTA creates the fashion; she decides how, when, and where to wear it.

## Message pillars
1. Own your choice: no rules about how daring a woman should be.
2. Make a statement: design that challenges predictable silhouettes.
3. Feel the difference: materials, fit, and finish deserve attention.
4. Your beauty, your terms: confidence is personal, not a performance.

## Product copy formula
Design detail + material/engineering proof + fit/use note.

Example: "A sculptural bodysuit with asymmetric mesh panels and adjustable straps. Built with [verified fabric composition] and [verified construction detail]. Review the fit notes and size guide to find your preferred fit."

Only publish material, comfort, support, sustainability, or performance claims after verification.

## Words to use
Daring, sculptural, intimate, considered, unapologetic, tactile, precise, provocative, confidence, statement, crafted, own.

## Avoid
Body-shaming language; claims that one body type is ideal; pressure to reveal more or wear lingerie in public; cheap shock language; degrading language; unsupported claims.

## Community principle
Never judge customers by how much they reveal. A woman wearing a subtle LENGTA piece is no less bold than a woman wearing the most dramatic design. Her choice is the point.
