# LENGTA — Product & Collection Direction

## Product universe
LENGTA shares the broad intimate-fashion universe of its twin brand, while applying its own more provocative design language.

Potential categories:
1. Lingerie sets: bras, briefs, thongs, high-waist and coordinated separates.
2. Bodysuits: sculptural, cut-out, sheer-panel, and layering-led designs.
3. Corsetry: structured fashion corsets, bustiers, and waist-focused silhouettes.
4. Nightwear: robes, slips, camisoles, and elevated sleep pieces.
5. Statement intimates: harness-inspired fashion details, strapping, and special-occasion pieces, with clear wear and care guidance.
6. Accessories: considered additions that complement the collection.

Final categories and specifications depend on supplier capability, fit testing, and customer validation.

## Design codes
- Asymmetry and unexpected linework.
- Strategic cut-outs and sheer/opaque contrast.
- Sculptural straps and refined hardware.
- Dramatic necklines and back details.
- Lace, mesh, satin, velvet accents, embroidery, and crystal embellishment.
- Strong color stories: Obsidian, Blood Wine, Bone, and selective seasonal statements.

Daring should be intentional and wearable. Every opening, strap, seam, and closure must be tested for fit, movement, comfort, and durability.

## Proposed collection architecture
### AFTER DARK
The signature LENGTA expression: black, oxblood, deep textures, confident silhouettes.

### NO APOLOGIES
The most experimental direction: asymmetric forms, cut-outs, statement straps, and fashion-forward layering.

### PRIVATE POWER
Elevated essentials with a sensual edge: refined cuts, considered support, and exceptional hand-feel.

### THE ART OF REVEAL
Sheer/opaque play, transparency, layering, and carefully engineered coverage.

These are proposed collection territories, not confirmed product lines.

## Product page requirements
Every product page should include front, back, side, and detail imagery; clear coverage and construction views; model height and garment size where consented and available; fit notes and support level; fabric composition and care; size guide; closure, adjustability, lining, and opacity details; and clear shipping, returns, and hygiene policies.

## Quality standards to establish
Fit testing across the intended size range; strap and closure strength; stretch and recovery; seam durability; hardware finish; skin-contact material and dye compliance for target markets; wash and care testing; consistent grading and quality inspection.

## Merchandising principle
The most daring design should never be the only reason to buy. Pair emotional appeal with credible proof: fit, finish, fabric, function, and service.
