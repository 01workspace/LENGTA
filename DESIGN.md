# LENGTA Storefront Design Brief

- Brand: LENGTA.
- Tagline and philosophy: OWN THE BEAUTY.
- Audience: adult women 18+ who choose daring, fashion-forward intimatewear.
- Feel: provocative, editorial, luxurious, self-possessed.
- Palette: Obsidian #09090B; Blood Wine #5A1026; Oxblood #32101C; Bone #F2EAE2; Antique Champagne #B89A72.
- Typography: Bodoni Moda display serif; Manrope for navigation and commerce details.
- Layout: image-led fashion-house storefront, wide negative space, sharp grid, fine rules, restrained metallic accents.
- Photography: supplied LENGTA campaign banners and matching studio/model imagery. Clearly adult models. Garment-first product presentation.
- Motion: restrained 150–250ms, reduced-motion aware.
- Header/footer: approved LENGTA logo artwork, not a replacement text wordmark.
- Asset provenance: uploaded `LENGTA website image.zip`. LENGTA assets selected by folder; explicitly VYRA- or SAXI-branded folders excluded.
- Status: images are now integrated in a draft website version. Product names/descriptions are editorial labels; final product specs, prices, size ranges, inventory, returns and checkout are not live.
